About Wildmark Field Co.

Wildmark builds gear lists for hunters who want to carry what they need and nothing they do not. Tell us the hunt and we size the kit to the game, the weather and your pack. Hunt prepared.

Weights and prices are estimates. Always check current regulations, seasons, license and tag requirements for your unit before you go. Weather and terrain change fast: tell someone your plan.
