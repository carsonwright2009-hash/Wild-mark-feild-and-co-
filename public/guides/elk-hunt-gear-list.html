Tips for this hunt

  • Elk are big animals. Plan for how you will get the meat out, not only how you will get in. Game bags and a way to carry heavy loads matter.
  • Camp gear (shelter, sleep system, stove, water filter) is where most of the cost and weight sits. Buy the big items once and buy them well.
  • Test your pack with a full load before the hunt. Find out what rubs, what rattles and what you never used.
