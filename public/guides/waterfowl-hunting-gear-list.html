Tips for this hunt

  • Split your gear in two piles: the one time buys (decoys, waders, blind, calls) and the every trip list (shells, license and stamps, hearing protection, headlamp, batteries). The second pile is the one people forget.
  • Non-toxic shot, such as steel, is required for waterfowl in the United States. Check your state and refuge rules for approved shot and any limits.
  • Check your wader seams and belt before opening day, not in the marsh.

Questions

What does it cost to get set up for duck hunting?

The example list below comes to about $1,826 if everything is bought new at mid-range prices. Decoys, waders and a blind are most of that, and most hunters build the kit up over a few seasons.

How heavy is a waterfowl kit?

This example kit weighs about 59 lb, mostly decoys and the blind. You will not carry it all at once on most hunts, so plan your trips from the truck.

What licenses do waterfowl hunters need?

Usually a state hunting license, state waterfowl permits and a federal Duck Stamp. Rules depend on your state and age, so confirm with your wildlife agency.

What do I pack every trip?

Shells, licenses and stamps, hearing protection, headlamp and spare batteries, a first-aid kit and a game strap. The builder lists these as high priority.
