Tips for this hunt

  • If you hunt with a dog, plan for the dog first: water, a first-aid kit, a blaze orange vest and a way to deal with thorns and cactus.
  • Blaze orange rules vary by state and species. Check your state rules before you head out.
  • Brush pants or chaps and gaiters pay for themselves the first time you push through heavy cover.

Questions

What does an upland bird hunt cost to gear up for?

The example list below comes to about $1,072 if everything is bought new at mid-range prices. Upland is one of the cheaper hunts to start, and many hunters already own much of this.

How heavy is an upland kit?

This example kit weighs about 23 lb including water and food, which is why a good vest or daypack is worth it.

Do I need gear for a dog?

Only if you hunt with one. The list includes dog water, a dog first-aid kit and a blaze orange vest, marked as for hunters with a dog.

What licenses do I need?

Usually a state hunting license, an upland game bird license, and any state stamps or permits. Confirm with your wildlife agency.
