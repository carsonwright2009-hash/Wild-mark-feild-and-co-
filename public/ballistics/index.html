How to use it

Your BC and bullet weight are on the ammo box or the bullet maker's website. Get your real muzzle velocity from a chronograph if you can, because the number on the box is often higher than what your barrel actually shoots. Set the zero to the distance you actually sighted in at.

The calculator uses a standard point-mass model with the G1 and G7 drag curves. Commercial apps use more detailed bullet data, so results can differ slightly, especially past 500 yards or with unusual bullets.
