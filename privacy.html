Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026. Draft template: have a lawyer review it before you rely on it.

What we collect

The gear builder runs in your browser. Your owned-gear list, check-in plan and journal stay on your device. If you sign in, we store your email address and your saved kits in our database, hosted by Supabase, so they sync across devices. If you subscribe to Premium, Stripe collects your name, email, billing details and payment card. We do not see or store your full card number.

What we receive from Stripe

We link your Stripe customer and subscription IDs to your account so the site knows Premium is active. Our server checks your subscription status with Stripe each time you visit.

Location field

Any location you type stays in your browser unless you copy a share link, which includes it.

Third parties

Stripe processes payments and Supabase hosts accounts and saved kits, each under its own privacy policy. If you use the live forecast, the place name you type is sent to Open-Meteo to look up the weather. Retail links open other websites that have their own policies. Some links may be affiliate links, and we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Your choices

Clear your browser data to remove everything stored on your device. To cancel Premium, use Manage subscription on the site. To ask about your data, email [your contact email].
